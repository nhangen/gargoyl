Coverage and limits

Missing or older reports need fresh evidence; nothing is inferred healthy. Account status is a coordinator report, not independent remote health. Account data stays inside the Mac app: the public loopback dashboard and export remain local-only, and fleet reads use no financial, model-name or provider-control endpoints.
