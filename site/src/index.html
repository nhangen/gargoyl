Principles

Evidence over assumptions

Every observation carries its source, timestamp, and freshness window. Stale, conflicting, or missing signals are labeled as such instead of being rounded up to “fine.”

Privacy by design

Prompts, completions, credentials, raw logs, and addresses stay on the host by default. Only allowlisted aggregate observations, tied to opaque host IDs, are meant to leave it.

Repairs under your control In development

Repair workflows are being designed as allowlisted, preflighted runbooks that target one host and wait for your confirmation. Nothing runs autonomously.

Actively being built.

Follow development on GitHub. Core telemetry and security pieces are in progress; validation against live hosts is still ahead.
